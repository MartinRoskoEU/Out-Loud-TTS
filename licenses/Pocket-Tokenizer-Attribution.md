- Supplied upstream policy: [Pocket-Prohibited-Use-Policy.txt](Pocket-Prohibited-Use-Policy.txt).

Tokenizers are native assets installed with the selected model, not bundled
vocabulary files in the installer. Exact tokenizer paths, repository revisions
and content hashes are recorded in the installed model inventory. Their original
bytes are preserved and are read by the official Pocket TTS Rust runtime.

Vocabulary data retains its asset license. Licenses for tokenizer software in
the Rust executable are covered separately by [runtime dependency notices](pocket-tts/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt).
Retain source/creator attribution, license identification, upstream notices and
any modification information when redistributing tokenizer assets. Out Loud's
MIT license does not relicense those assets or imply creator endorsement.
